### `dropdown_click_target_quirk`

**Discovered:** 2026-04-08

**Surface (App2):** App2 dropdowns open on a click anywhere within the widget.

**QS limitation:** QS ParameterDropDownControl opens only when the operator clicks the inner grey bar; clicking the visible edge does nothing — a usability footgun App2 sidesteps by design.

**QS limitation:** QS SheetTextBox supports only its constrained XML vocabulary (inline color/size/bg/font, b/i/s/u, block align, ql-indent-N nesting) — rich operator-help content must be flattened or linked out.
